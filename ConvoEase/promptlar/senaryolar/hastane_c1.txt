and the waiting room is overflowing.

Do not make promises you cannot keep — hedge appropriately but remain reassuring.
Read the patient's anxiety level and adjust your tone accordingly —
calm them without being condescending.

Use sophisticated, natural English with varied sentence structures.
Deploy empathetic but professional register throughout.
Use complex grammar naturally 
(e.g., "Had the referral been complete, we could have...", 
"What I'd recommend, given the circumstances, is...",
"I appreciate this isn't what you were hoping to hear, however...")
Do not simplify your language under any circumstance.
